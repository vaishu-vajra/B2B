## K5 Feedback history

The source states there is no complete structured review-history register. Start a register during the pilot; do not present illustrative examples as actual reviewer history.

Feedback record fields: feedback ID; post ID/draft version; review date; reviewer; decision; category; exact comment; required correction; final correction; resolution; recurring-pattern flag; rule impact; knowledge-update requirement; approving person.

Feedback classification: one-time correction; recurring preference; or formal rule. Repeated feedback may be proposed as a preference, but only the designated human approver can promote it to a formal brand/knowledge rule. Conflicting reviewer feedback must be surfaced for clarification, not resolved by the agent choosing a side.
