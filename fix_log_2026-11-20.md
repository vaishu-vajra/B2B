## 2. Publishing dates from a date range and post count

### What Was There
The Vajra pilot used six fixed weekly publication dates.

### What Is the Issue
Changing the publishing window or post count required changing the fixed schedule.

### What Is the Fix
Added publishing start date, publishing end date, and post count to the form for Brief Qualifier and Content Planner. The API spreads posts as evenly as possible across the inclusive range. For two or more posts, it uses both endpoints. For one post, it uses the start date. It rejects a reversed range or more posts than available dates. The active brief supplies the defaults when the form is blank. The draft stays due five calendar days before publishing, and final-copy approval is due two calendar days before publishing.

## 3. One calendar approval and drafting the entire batch

### What Was There
The calendar showed individual drafting approvals, and Post Writer took one selected item.

### What Is the Issue
The approver could clear one item while the rest of the calendar still waited. The writer could also produce only one draft after a calendar review.

### What Is the Fix
Record one explicit approval for all posts on the exact calendar version. Partial, pending, missing, or outdated approval holds the entire batch. A material calendar change returns it to Pending. Once the calendar is approved and all item briefs are complete, Post Writer drafts every post in calendar order. Calendar rows show brief completeness. Final-copy approval remains a separate decision.
