## Use rules from source

- Describe capabilities accurately; the PDF says service capabilities, public website thought leadership, approved employee expertise, approved milestones/events, and public client work within its disclosure scope may be used (p. 30).
- Client names/results may be used only through an approved proof-point record and with explicit disclosure permission (pp. 23–24, 30–32; final map p. 94).
- If evidence is missing, ask for the supporting report, timeframe, client permission and approved wording; use a non-quantified alternative only if it is accurate and within approved capability wording (p. 32).
- Do not publish confidential client information, unlaunched offers as available, unverified statistics, guaranteed outcomes, unsupported AI capabilities, legal/security guarantees, or unsupported superiority/leadership claims (pp. 22–25).
- The source says no single mandatory corporate disclaimer is established; use a disclaimer only where the subject requires one and obtain subject-matter owner approval (p. 23).

## Proof-point register requirements

Each actual proof point must have: claim ID, exact statement, source document/link and location, evidence, permitted wording, scope/reuse permission, client/name/logo permission where applicable, approval owner/date, validity/expiry, and status/history. The PDF's example of a 35% qualified-lead increase is explicitly illustrative, **not an actual Vajra result** (pp. 31–32); it is not entered as a Vajra claim.
