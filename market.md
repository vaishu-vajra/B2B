## K6.1 Competitor record fields

For each approved competitor, capture: unique ID; official name; official website and LinkedIn URL; headquarters; primary markets; publicly stated services; communicated industries and audiences; positioning; recurring content pillars; formats; observed publishing frequency over a defined period; dated engagement observations; public proof points; possible differentiation opportunity; evidence URL; and observation date.

## K6.2 Research questions

- Do competitors position as agencies, consultants, technology partners or growth partners?
- Do they frame AI as a feature or as a change in marketing operations?
- Do they show business outcomes or focus on deliverables?
- Do they publish original frameworks and perspectives?
- Are case studies specific and credible?
- Which buyer personas do they address, and what content gaps may Vajra address?

## K6.3 Operating constraints

Use public information only; record source URL and observation date; do not copy distinctive content/creative; do not claim Vajra is superior without evidence; do not infer commercial success from social engagement; revalidate observations regularly.
