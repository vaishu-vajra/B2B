## K1.2 Voice, tone and writing style

Source-described attributes (p. 19):

- Strategic: connect marketing to business goals, growth, revenue, customer experience and operations.
- Insightful: explain what is changing and why it matters to business leaders.
- Practical: use frameworks, examples, recommendations and actionable takeaways.
- Confident without boastfulness or unsupported claims.
- Human: recognise people, teams, expertise, collaboration and culture.
- Forward-looking on AI and emerging technology, with balance.
- Conversational and accessible; avoid unnecessarily academic or technical writing.

Writing rules in source (p. 19): concise paragraphs; meaningful hooks; lead with a business problem, observation, question or insight; explain technical concepts accessibly; use emojis selectively; avoid excessive jargon, exaggerated claims and generic AI phrases; end with a relevant CTA, question or next step; use relevant hashtags selectively.

- Spelling: Indian/British English is specified **subject to final brand-style decision**. `[P]` Pending decision (final file map p. 89).

## K1.4 Do's, don'ts and visual guidance

Source rules (pp. 21–22): connect ideas to business impact; use verified examples; show expertise through useful insights; use approved service descriptions; tag employees only when relevant and approved; use current credible sources; keep client stories within disclosure limits; make CTAs relevant. Do not publish generic advice without Vajra's point of view, invent results or testimonials, claim market leadership without evidence, promote uncleared launches, tag without checking permission, repeat unverified research, expose confidential information, or make every post sales-heavy.
