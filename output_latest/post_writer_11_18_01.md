## Draft copy

Before choosing an AI task, decide where a person takes over.

Picture one marketing task moving from a brief to a draft:
- the team sets the input
- AI produces a first pass
- a person checks it against the brief
- an owner decides what happens next

That sequence makes human responsibility part of the workflow design, instead of a fix after the handoff breaks.

Where are you connecting AI to a marketing workflow?
