## Draft copy

Where does work go after an AI-assisted task is complete?

A handoff needs an owner just like any other step.

A quick workflow check can start with four questions:
- where the work begins and ends
- what information moves between steps
- who checks the output
- who receives the next handoff

Mapping these points puts responsibility and human judgment into the workflow discussion.

Where are you connecting AI to a marketing workflow?
