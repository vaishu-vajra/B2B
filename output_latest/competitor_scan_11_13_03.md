## 4. Competitive analysis

### Observations
- Social Beat communicates growth through a combined media, creativity and technology model; recent topics include AI in paid media and search.
- Schbang’s visible feed emphasizes integrated campaigns and culture-led creative, with a recent tier-list post as an explanatory format.
- Performics India emphasizes performance marketing, data and media effectiveness; its recent AI post frames AI as a way to reduce repetitive work and create more room for human thinking.
- Public feed coverage is incomplete for all profiles, and YAAP’s page could not be inspected in this run. Publishing-frequency comparison is therefore unavailable.

### Interpretations
- The observed competitors describe AI within wider marketing work: media buying and search at Social Beat, creative/media/technology integration at Schbang, and performance operations and staff enablement at Performics India.
- Schbang’s visible examples lean toward campaign storytelling, while the other two feeds show more explicit AI/media-operation commentary in the sampled posts. This describes only the sampled public material, not a full editorial strategy.
