## Draft copy

Who owns the handoff when an AI-assisted task moves to the next person?

That choice shapes how work moves across a marketing team.

Before adding AI to a process, map:
- where the work starts and ends
- what information passes between steps
- who reviews the output
- who owns the next action

Clear handoffs give people a shared way to see where AI fits and where human judgment belongs.

Where are you connecting AI to a marketing workflow?
