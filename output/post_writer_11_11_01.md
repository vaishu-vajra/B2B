## Draft copy

AI experiments can create activity. Connected workflows make that activity part of how marketing gets done.

Start with the work, not the tool. Choose a real marketing task, identify the information it depends on, and decide where human judgment should guide or review the output.

That gives an experiment a clearer path into everyday practice—without assuming every use case needs automation.

Where are you connecting AI to a marketing workflow?
