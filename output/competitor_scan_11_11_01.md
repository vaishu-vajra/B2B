## 4. Competitive analysis

### Observations
- Social Beat’s in-window public material connects AI with media planning, measurement, and creative production.
- Schbang’s October roundup emphasizes campaign execution across brands, formats, creators, seasonal moments, and social-led work; its recent LinkedIn feed also promotes planning calendars and campaign examples.
- Performics India’s recent public content includes AI capability-building inside teams alongside performance marketing and learning stories.
- The dated D&B survey release describes rising AI investment intent alongside limited data readiness among respondents.

### Interpretations
- The three competitors communicate AI through different proof routes: connected media/creative systems, campaign and format breadth, and workforce or performance capability-building.
- Across the sampled public material, AI is often tied to operating processes, data, creative production, or team learning rather than presented only as a standalone tool. This is an interpretation of the cited examples, not a full-market conclusion.
- The D&B release suggests an industry tension between investment ambition and readiness to scale. This is an inference from the survey publisher’s reported results, not an independently verified forecast.

### Hypothesis for Vajra — unvalidated
- A useful content angle may be to show how a marketing team connects one AI experiment to a real workflow: task, required information, human decision point, and measurement. The angle fits Vajra’s internal positioning around AI-enabled marketing and connected growth work, but current public service wording and proof remain subject to owner confirmation.
- **Evidence needed before making company-specific claims:** an approved example of a Vajra workflow, confirmation of the capability and its public status, supporting evidence for any outcome, and any required client permission. Until then, keep the idea as general thought leadership and do not claim superiority or proven results.
